## About Me

I work at the intersection of **computer science, artificial intelligence, research, education, and software engineering**. As a faculty member and R&D project leader, I turn interdisciplinary ideas into practical platforms, intelligent systems, and digital tools that serve institutions and communities.

- 🔬 Leading and contributing to **Computing + X research and development**
- 🧠 Exploring **AI, intelligent systems, machine learning, and applied generative AI**
- 🛠️ Building production-ready **web, mobile, desktop, data, and API solutions**
- 🤝 Open to collaboration on **interdisciplinary research and community-centered innovation**
- 👨‍🏫 Connecting teaching, mentorship, research, and real-world software development
- 🙏 Religion: **Omnist**
- ⚡ I love languages—both **human and machine** 🤖

## Teaching & Academic Work

My academic work brings together **Computer Science instruction, research mentorship, software engineering practice, and applied AI**. I aim to help students move from foundational concepts to responsible, useful solutions—especially projects that address needs in education, agriculture, culture, governance, and local communities.

Areas of interest include:

- Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Intelligent Systems
- Software Engineering, Web/Mobile Development & API Design
- Research Methods, Data Analytics & Project Development
- Computing + X, Human-Centered Technology & Digital Heritage
